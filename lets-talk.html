Common questions

Before you ask…

Is the EHFD program medical advice?
No. EHFD shares general educational information and personal experience. Always work with your doctor or a qualified health professional before changing your diet, exercise or medication — especially if you take medicines for blood sugar or blood pressure.
Do I need to buy the course to get started?
Not at all. Start with the free Health Journal, our YouTube videos and the Facebook community. When you're ready for a structured, step-by-step program, the Udemy course is there for you.
Who is this for?
Adults — especially those over 45 — who have been told they have prediabetes, are carrying extra weight, or simply want to eat better and feel healthier. Our community spans New Zealand, Australia and the United States.
How do I get the free Health Journal?
Fill in the form on this page (or the Home page) with your email address, and we'll send you a link to download the journal. It's our gift — no payment, no catch.
I take medication. Is this still safe for me?
Many people on this journey take medication — but changes to diet and activity can affect blood sugar and blood pressure, so your medication needs may change. That's a good thing to manage with your doctor, not on your own.
