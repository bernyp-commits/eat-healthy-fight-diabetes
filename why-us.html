The evidence

The science is on your side

These aren't motivational slogans — they're findings from major studies and national health bodies.

Lifestyle change cuts risk by more than half

In a landmark US prevention study, participants who lost 5–7% of their body weight through healthy eating and activity reduced their risk of developing type 2 diabetes by 58%. The US CDC sums it up simply: eating healthy and being more active can cut your risk in half.

Source: CDC — Prediabetes: Could It Be You?

Type 2 diabetes can be put into remission

In the UK's DiRECT trial, 46% of participants who followed a structured weight-loss program were in remission from type 2 diabetes after one year — and some remained in remission at five years. The key driver was sustained weight loss.

Source: Diabetes UK — Research on remission

Most type 2 diabetes is preventable

Diabetes Australia reports that almost 60% of type 2 diabetes cases can be delayed or prevented with changes to diet and lifestyle — and that around 2 million Australians currently have prediabetes.

Source: Diabetes Australia — Facts about diabetes

Prediabetes is common — and often silent

More than 2 in 5 US adults have prediabetes and 8 in 10 don't know it. In New Zealand, more than one in four adults has prediabetes. Catching it early is the whole game.

Sources: CDC · HRI New Zealand

Research summaries are provided for general information. Individual results vary, and any changes to diet, exercise or medication should be made with your doctor.
