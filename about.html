The founder's story

From prediabetes diagnosis to living proof

Like many people, I didn't feel sick. Life was busy, the kilos crept on quietly, and a routine check-up delivered words I never expected: prediabetes.

That moment became a turning point. Instead of extremes, I focused on changing my mindset first — how I thought about food, movement and my own health. Small, consistent changes followed. Weight came off. Energy returned. Blood sugar improved.

Along the way I shared what I was learning in the Eat Healthy Fight Diabetes community. The response was overwhelming — so many people over 45 facing exactly the same wake-up call. That response became this website, the course, and everything else we do.

If I can do it, you can too. Not because it's easy — but because it's doable, one healthy meal at a time.
